I'm Gareth Western — a software developer and consultant based in Norway,
currently working as a tech lead on data platforms.

platforms and the small integrations that hold systems together. These days

maintained at a leisurely pace ever since.

This site is where I write up things I've figured out — usually so future me
can find them again. Mostly Azure, Jenkins and GitHub Actions, and mostly
whenever I remember the blog exists.
