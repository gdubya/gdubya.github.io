I'm Gareth Western — a **Data Platform Architect and IT Consultant** based in Norway,
specializing in designing and implementing scalable, enterprise-grade cloud data solutions.
I bridge the gap between complex software engineering principles and modern data architecture.

I've spent most of my working life somewhere around build pipelines, cloud
platforms and the small integrations that hold systems together. These days
that mostly means data platforms and Databricks; earlier on it meant a lot of
Java programming and CI/CD. Somewhere in the middle I wrote the
[MQTT Notification Plugin for Jenkins][mqtt-plugin], released in 2013 and
maintained at a leisurely pace ever since.
